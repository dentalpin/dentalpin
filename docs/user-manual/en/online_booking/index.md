# Online booking requests

An administrator must enable online booking and configure email and a public confirmation page. Availability follows the working hours of the professional chosen in the settings; appointments of other professionals do not block it. The emailed link confirms a request; reception decides whether to accept or reject it. Confirming does not create an appointment.

A confirmed request holds its slot until reception decides or its start time. Before acceptance, the system checks appointments again and reports conflicts for reception to resolve. Rejected and expired requests are deleted after 30 days; contact information on accepted requests is erased after 90 days.

The public page lets visitors choose a visit type and time, provide contact details and complete ALTCHA. The email link requires selecting “Confirm request”; opening it does not create or confirm an appointment.

The calendar highlights dates with availability and lets visitors move between weeks. Phone and email are required. Authorized staff can edit the logo, color and page text in “Public page settings”.
